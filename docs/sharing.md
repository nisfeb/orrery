# Sharing a body with another ship

A body and its observations can be shared with another ship: read mode mirrors what you know onto their ship, edit mode also carries what they observe back onto yours. The unit is one body. Nothing else on the ship is visible to them.

## How it works

- `POST /apps/orrery/api/share` with `{"id": "person/sarah", "ship": "~feb", "mode": "read"}` (or `"edit"`) records the share in `shares.json`, lets `~feb` read the body's directory (a usergroup named `orrery-person.sarah` with one peek grant), and pokes an offer into `~feb`'s inbox. The answer's `notified` is false only when the other ship vetoed the poke or nacked it. A poke that timed out answers true: grubbery's remote acks are unobservable and a poke that times out usually landed. The first offer assumes the other ship installed orrery at the standard desk path; a ship that did not answers `notified` false.
- On `~feb`, `GET /apps/orrery/api/shares` lists `offers`, `accepted` and `shares` (what this ship shares out). `POST /api/accept` with `{"host": "~wex", "id": "person/sarah"}` takes an offer. A local body that already carries the offered ship is where it lands, whatever either side calls it. Failing that, a body whose ship is `~feb` itself lands on `person/me`, the host's own `person/me` lands on `person/wex` (its name without the sig), and any other body lands under its own id. The body is created with the offered name and ship only when it is not there yet. `POST /api/decline` drops an offer.
- The follower runs every five minutes, on every accept and on `POST /api/sync`. It reads each accepted body whole from the host and submits the host's own observations to the local writer with `by` set to the host and `source` `{"kind": "ship", "id": "~wex/<host observation id>"}`. A row the host retracts is retracted here.
- What crosses the wire is the whole directory. The peer ship reads every row the host holds on that body, with its source pointer, its actor, its note, and the rows the host mirrored from third ships. What orrery stores on the peer is narrower: only the host's own rows, and their source pointer is replaced by the host's grub name. A third ship's rows are not stored, and the peer's owner can still read them with a plain remote peek.
- In edit mode the follower also sends the local observations on that body (the ones not mirrored from a ship) to the host's inbox, where they land with `by` set to the sender and the same source shape. Retractions travel the same way. The host's inbox checks the share record before it applies anything; the sender is the transport's, never the payload's.
- `DELETE /apps/orrery/api/share/person/sarah/~feb` removes the ship from the record and the group and tells the other ship, which drops the accepted row. Mirrored observations stay on both sides, still naming their source.

## What to know

- The ask grows by poke on `/sys/gall/`, `/sys/behn/` and `/sys/ames/registry`, peek on `/sys/ames/usergroups/` and `/sys/ames/ships/`, make on `/sys/ames/usergroups/`. Refuse them and everything else keeps working; sharing is off.
- `{"ref"}` values travel verbatim. They name bodies on the ship that observed them. One that names the receiving body itself is refused here and noted once in the ship-traffic log (`/tr/inbox`).
- The name and ship of a shared body are copied when the accept creates it here; a body that already exists keeps its own name. Aliases are not carried; observations are.
- Every accepted row carries `last` (the last attempt, successful or not) and `error` (empty, or why the host could not be read or would not take the edits). Deleting the local body the share lands on stops the pass with `the shared body does not exist here any more`. In edit mode the host's acknowledgement means it received the rows, not that it kept them: rows sent after the host narrowed the share are dropped there and noted in its ship-traffic log (`/tr/inbox`). A push that timed out counts as pushed, since the host answered the peek a moment earlier.
- A share can be narrowed by the host at any time and it applies at once, and a wider offer still waiting in the inbox goes with it, so it cannot be accepted afterwards. Widening one (read to edit) is a new offer: nothing of yours leaves the ship until you accept it.
- A ship that is down does not stall the follower: a peek or a poke gives up after thirty seconds and the row records it.
- Offers, revokes and edits from other ships are logged in `/tr/inbox`, a ring of 500 of their own, so nothing a stranger sends can push the owner's audit log out of `/tr/log`. It is read through the ball browser at `GET /grubbery/ball/apps/shell.shell/desks/orrery.desk/desk/data/orrery.orrery_app/tr/inbox?raw=1`.
- Retention applies to mirrored rows too: a row older than `retention_days` by the later of when it was true and when the ship recorded it can be culled here and mirrored again from the host, and the map of what was pushed grows for the life of a share.
- A carried row's id is a hash over the fields orrery stores, its source pointer among them, so the same claim read twice makes one grub here.
- A share carries the body whole: every attribute on it, the sensitive ones included, with its notes, its actors and its source pointers. The read grant is a grant on a directory and cannot filter by attribute, so a body holding facts you would not share is not a body to share.
- The push is the one direction that can be filtered, and it is: in edit mode an attribute named in `policy.sensitive` is never sent to the host, live or retracted.
- Deleting a shared body drops its share record and empties its group, so the ships it was shared with lose the peek with the body.
